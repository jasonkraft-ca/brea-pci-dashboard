Two different averages appear on this page, deliberately. Each street's own score (used in the list and histogram below) is the pavement-area-weighted average of its own segments — so a street's rating reflects its longer, wider blocks more than a short stub. The "Simple avg" stat card above then treats every street equally regardless of size. The Citywide Weighted PCI panel instead weights every one of the 1,361 surveyed segments by its own pavement area across the whole network, which is how the PMP computes its official network-level figures — and is why it comes out lower (81.6) than the simple per-street average (84.0) here: unlike Placentia, Brea's numerous short, high-scoring cul-de-sacs and local streets don't carry enough pavement area to offset its lower-scoring MPAH arterial corridors once area is factored in. The "Planned" column reflects the earliest fiscal year each street has scheduled work in Appendix A's 7-year Forecast Maintenance & Rehabilitation (FMR) report (FY 2025-26 through 2031-32); a street can have multiple segments scheduled across different years, and this shows the soonest. Streets marked "Not yet scheduled" fall outside the current 7-year funding window — typically because they still score well enough that other streets take priority, though a few lower-scoring streets are also absent, likely reflecting funding constraints rather than a full program. Bands follow the standard 0–100 StreetSaver scale: 0–25 Failed, 26–50 Poor, 51–70 Fair, 71–85 Satisfactory, 86–100 Good. Figures sourced from Attachment F of the City's 2025 Citywide Pavement Management Plan (Final Report, May 28, 2025), as presented to the Brea City Council on June 17, 2025.
